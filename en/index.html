EasyMaint · free CMMS

All of your plant's maintenance, in a web browser

EasyMaint installs with one command on a computer in the plant. Technicians use it from their phones; managers follow costs and KPIs.

AGPL-3.0 licenseEnglish · FrançaisWorks without InternetWindows · Linux · Docker
  • Maintenance work: requests, work orders, mobile technician screen, validation and costs.
  • Preventive: calendar and meter-based plans, automatic generation, schedule.
  • Condition monitoring: vibration and oil analysis, thresholds, alerts, import and sensor API.
  • Stores: stock at average cost, parts issued to work orders with signed slips, purchase requests.
  • Costs and KPIs: budget per unit, MTBF, MTTR, availability, monthly PDF report.
  • Commissioning: data migration through Excel imports, nightly automatic backups.

About

Software written from the shop floor and the classroom

JugurthaSoft is run by Jamil KHALFALLAH, a mechanical engineer (1998). From 1999 to 2005 he worked in maintenance planning for an offshore oil company, on mechanical maintenance.

Since then he has taught industrial maintenance at a higher institute of technological studies, especially condition-based maintenance, vibration monitoring and non-destructive testing, and in recent years mechatronics.

Each program answers a need seen in a plant or in a lab session: running a workshop's maintenance, learning to read a vibration spectrum, simulating an eddy-current or ultrasonic inspection.
